Privacy Policy

VTOP Chennai App • Updated August 2026
Privacy First

1. Client-Side Architecture

VTOP Chennai is built with a strict client-side first philosophy. The application communicates directly with official VIT servers (VTOP and Moodle) from your device.

We do not operate intermediate servers that capture, log, or store your student credentials.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All academic and profile information fetched from VTOP is stored locally and securely on your phone:

  • Login Session & Cookies: Saved locally to maintain your session without asking for passwords repeatedly.
  • Academic Records: Attendance, course timetable, grades, exam schedules, and receipts are cached in an encrypted local SQLite database for fast offline viewing.
  • Hostel Schedules: Mess menus, room numbers, and laundry drop-off dates are stored in local device preferences.

3. Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Notifications & Alarms: Used solely to notify you before scheduled classes and upcoming examinations.
  • Do Not Disturb (Smart DND): Used strictly during class hours to silence your phone and restore previous ringer settings when class ends.
  • Internet Access: Required to sync your latest attendance, marks, and timetable directly from VTOP and Moodle.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses minimal standard services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics: Collects anonymous app performance and crash diagnostics to fix bugs. No student registration numbers or passwords are ever transmitted.
  • GitHub Releases: Used to check for official application updates and download new releases securely.

5. Your Control & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. Tapping Sign Out from the Profile screen instantly clears all local cookies, cached database entries, and credentials from your device.
